How it works

Merge sort is divide and conquer: split the range in half, sort each half recursively, then merge. Merging copies the range into a buffer and repeatedly takes the smaller front element of the two halves, writing it back into the array.

Time O(n log n) in every case · extra space O(n) · stable (equal elements keep their order).
